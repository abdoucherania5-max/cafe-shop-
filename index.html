Our Story

A sanctuary dedicated to female craft and unhurried grace.

Founded by two lifelong friends in search of a quieter space to write and connect, Lumière was born out of a desire for intentional hospitality. We set out to reimagine the cafe experience away from rushed lines and sterile industrial corners.

Every bean we grind is ethically sourced from female smallholder cooperatives in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. Our botanical syrups are infused in-house from organic damask roses, dried lavender blossoms, and green cardamom, creating warm aromas that welcome you the second you step inside.
